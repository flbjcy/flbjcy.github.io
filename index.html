Projects

Research Preprint on arXiv

RAFP

Rare-region Adversarial Fingerprinting for LLM Lineage Verification

A fingerprinting method for distributed, multi-agent LLM frameworks that defends against adversarial model theft. RAFP embeds identifiable signals in rare regions of a model's input space and comes with theoretical robustness guarantees for verifying model lineage. Developed at Columbia's Software Systems Laboratory under Prof. Junfeng Yang.

Transformer LM From Scratch

A language model built end to end, then made fast

A Transformer-based language model implemented from the ground up, including a BPE tokenizer, attention blocks, and decoder, followed by systems-level optimization: profiling forward/backward/optimizer passes, writing a fused FlashAttention-2 Triton kernel, and distributing training across GPUs with DDP, optimizer sharding, and FSDP.
