FindMyCharlie

A community map for Boston’s collectible CharlieCards

I built FindMyCharlie to help fellow transit enthusiasts find all eight card designs without scrolling through hundreds of Reddit comments. It maps reports by station and machine, combines hourly Reddit reviews with community submissions, and hides sightings superseded by reports of empty or broken machines.

RAFP

Rare-region Adversarial Fingerprinting for LLM Lineage Verification

A fingerprinting method that defends a language model against adversarial theft. RAFP embeds identifiable signals in rare regions of the model's input space and comes with theoretical robustness guarantees for verifying model lineage. Developed at Columbia's Software Systems Laboratory under Prof. Junfeng Yang.

A language model built end to end, then made fast
